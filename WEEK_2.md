#  Week 2 Sprint: Random Variables & Common Probability Distributions

Welcome to Week 2 of the **ML/AI GRIND** weekly sprint! After building a strong foundation in probability and Bayes’ rule last week, we are zooming in on a critical pillar for machine learning and modeling: **Random Variables, Probability Functions, and Common Distributions**.

---

###  Weekly Learning Objectives
1. Understand the difference between discrete and continuous random variables, PMFs, PDFs, and CDFs.
2. Master mathematical expectation, variance, and covariance.
3. Deep-dive into core distributions used in machine learning (Gaussian/Normal, Bernoulli, Binomial, Poisson, and Exponential) and *why* models assume them.

###  Weekend Mock Interview Guide & Format
Over this coming Saturday and Sunday, we will conduct our peer mock interviews based on the rotation tracked in the [October Mock Interview Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/10fksoU7Ws7NrxaoaqcJ5gni_97uiE9UDyP50sxTdnZU/edit?gid=633550724#gid=633550724).

#### How the Mock Interview Works (30–45 mins per session):
1. **The Question (10 mins):** The Interviewer presents a scenario involving distributions, expected value calculations, or maximum likelihood estimation (e.g., "Why do we use Mean Squared Error assuming Gaussian errors?").
2. **The Walkthrough (15 mins):** The Interviewee restates the problem, defines the random variables, outlines the mathematical or coding approach, and addresses underlying statistical assumptions.
3. **Feedback & Discussion (10 mins):** Constructive feedback on clarity, statistical rigor, and application reasoning.
