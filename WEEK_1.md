# Week 1 Sprint: Mathematics & Statistics (Applied Probability & Bayes’ Rule)

Welcome to Week 1 of the **ML/AI GRIND** weekly sprint! After an overwhelming vote by the team, our focus for this week is diving deep into **Applied Probability, Conditional Probability, and Bayes’ Rule** tailored for data science and machine learning interviews.

---

### Weekly Learning Objectives
1. Move past rote formula memorization and master real-world probability reasoning.
2. Understand how Bayes' Theorem applies directly to classification models (e.g., Naive Bayes logic, posterior probability updating).
3. Handle conditional probability edge cases (such as handling zero-probability scenarios with smoothing techniques).

### Practical Group Task
* Build a lightweight Python script from scratch (using only `numpy` or `pandas`) that computes conditional probabilities and applies Bayes' Theorem to a sample binary classification dataset. 
* Focus on cleanly writing out your assumptions, verifying edge cases, and checking mathematical units.

###  Weekend Mock Interview Guide & Format
Over this coming Saturday and Sunday, we will conduct our peer mock interviews based on the pairings tracked in the [Mock Interview Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/10fksoU7Ws7NrxaoaqcJ5gni_97uiE9UDyP50sxTdnZU/edit?usp=sharing).

#### How the Mock Interview Works (30–45 mins per session):
1. **The Question (10 mins):** The Interviewer presents a practical probability or Bayes' rule scenario/coding problem to the Interviewee.
2. **The Walkthrough (15 mins):** The Interviewee restates the problem, states assumptions, outlines a step-by-step mathematical or code-based solution, and discusses complexity/edge cases.
3. **Feedback & Discussion (10 mins):** Constructive feedback on communication clarity, mathematical rigor, and coding practices.
